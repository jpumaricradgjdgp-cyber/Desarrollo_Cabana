# Foto del diseño de Cabaña

Archivo usado por la aplicación: `frontend/assets/cabin-blue.jpg`.

La foto se generó con la herramienta integrada **imagegen** y se exportó a JPEG para que el sitio cargue con menos peso. Representa una cabaña entre pinos al anochecer, con tonos azules y luz cálida en las ventanas.

Prompt final usado para generar la imagen:

> Create a premium photorealistic landscape photograph for the background of Cabaña, a cinema streaming interface with a navy blue and vivid blue brand. A beautiful cozy timber cabin nestled in tall pine trees at blue hour, atmospheric deep midnight blue sky, cool blue forest and distant mountain silhouettes, small amber glow through cabin windows, natural authentic wood and stone materials, cinematic but believable photography. Cabin near the center-right, striking silhouette legible when cropped vertically, tranquil inviting mood. Rich navy and cobalt tones dominate, restrained warm window light. Landscape 16:9 composition, uncluttered negative space upper left to support navigation overlays. No text, no logo, no people, no brick wall, no interface elements. High quality architectural travel photography, natural fine detail, not an illustration.
